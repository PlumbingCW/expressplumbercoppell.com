When a failed water heater cannot wait

A heater that leaks from the tank body is done, and every hour it feeds water to the floor adds damage. Water in a closet next to carpet, or in a garage near stored boxes, is worth an emergency call.

Shut the cold supply valve above the tank, switch off the breaker for electric units, and turn a gas heater to off. Drain nothing yourself unless we tell you to. If the room is wet near electrical gear, stay out and call.

What a replacement visit involves

We confirm the size, fuel type and venting, then drain and disconnect the old tank. The new heater goes in with fresh supply connectors, a relief valve and a discharge pipe, and an expansion tank where the setup calls for one.

Gas heaters get a leak check at every joint and a draft test at the vent. Electric units get tested at the breaker and the thermostat. Before we leave, we show you the shutoff valve, the temperature setting and the drain valve so you know the layout.

Heat pump and electric options in Texas homes

A hybrid heat pump heater pulls heat from the air around it and uses far less electricity than a standard electric tank. It needs open air space, a drain for condensate and a few feet of clearance. A cramped closet is a poor fit, a garage is usually fine.

Ask about it if your home is all electric or if you are replacing an old electric tank. Check with your electric provider for any rebate before you buy, and we will help you read the label.
