When a burst pipe is an emergency: first ten minutes

Close the main shutoff, usually near the front exterior wall or in the garage. If you cannot find it or it will not turn, close the meter valve at the street box only if you can reach it safely, or tell the dispatcher and we will talk you through it.

Open a low faucet to drain the lines, and switch off power to wet areas at the breaker. If the water heater is nearby, turn off its power or gas so the tank does not heat with no inflow. Take photos of the water and any damage before cleanup for your insurer.

What the 2021 freeze taught Coppell homeowners

In February 2021 a deep freeze hit North Texas, and Oncor reported about 11,500 Coppell residents without power on the Monday morning of the outage. Coppell ISD closed for a week. Pipes in attics, garages and exterior walls froze and split, and many of the leaks only appeared after thawing.

The lesson is practical. Know where your main valve is before a freeze, insulate exposed lines, open cabinet doors under sinks on hard freeze nights, and let a faucet drip. If a pipe has frozen but not burst, do not use a flame to thaw it. Warm air from a hair dryer is safer.

After the repair: drying and next steps

We repair the pipe, restore water, check for further leaks with a pressure test and leave you with the pipe and fitting identified in plain words. Water damage cleanup and drywall are a separate trade, and we suggest you call your insurer early.

If the burst was in an area with several old fittings, ask about replacing the weak run before the next cold snap. For a broader look, leak detection can find any other slow leaks the freeze caused.

Heater and appliance connections that burst

Failed washing machine hoses, water heater connectors and refrigerator ice maker lines cause many flood calls. They are inexpensive parts that rarely get replaced. A rubber hose over ten years old is a risk worth swapping for a braided line with a new shutoff valve.

If your water heater flexible connector split, see water heater repair for the related fixes.
