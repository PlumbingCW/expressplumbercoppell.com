When a clogged drain is an emergency

A single slow bathroom sink can wait until morning. Water coming up in a tub while the washing machine drains, gurgling toilets, or sewage in a floor drain cannot. Those signs usually point to the main line, and every flush adds more water to a pipe that has nowhere to send it.

While you wait for us, stop using sinks, showers, dishwashers and washing machines. Move rugs and electronics away from the lowest drain in the house. If wastewater is near an outlet or power strip, leave that area alone and tell the dispatcher when you call.

Why drains clog in Coppell homes

Coppell grew quickly after DFW Airport opened in 1974, and almost all of its residential land was built out by 2000. That puts most homes at 25 to 50 years old. Drain lines that age collect scale, grease film and the occasional offset joint where a root or a settled pipe catches debris.

Soil movement matters too. Clay soil across the Dallas and Fort Worth region swells when it is wet and shrinks when it dries, according to the Dallas County Master Gardeners. Over many seasons that movement can nudge buried drain lines, and a pipe that sags holds water and waste at the low point. The first sign is often a drain that clears for a week and then slows again.

Kitchen sink clogs and what really causes them

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathrooms. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe, grabs coffee grounds and food scraps, and builds a thick wall that narrows the line a little more each week. Garbage disposals grind food small but do not stop grease from coating the pipe past the trap.

A cable can punch a hole through that wall and restore flow for the moment. When the pipe is heavily coated, hydro jetting cleans the full pipe wall and keeps the drain open longer. If the disposal itself is leaking or jammed, a separate fix is needed, and our garbage disposal repair page covers that.

Main line clogs and the cleanout

Coppell describes a residential sewer cleanout as a capped pipe that gives access to the sewer line so blockages can be removed. It is usually near the house and often sits in a flower bed. Knowing where yours is saves time on an emergency call.

If you see wastewater backing up and you are not sure whether the blockage is on your side of the property line, the city's Public Works Operations line is 972-462-5150, and the city says an on-call technician returns after-hours calls. We still clear private-side stoppages, and we will tell you plainly when the problem is in the city main. For longer runs of buried pipe, our sewer line cleaning service handles the main.

Shower, tub and drain line leaks

A tub that drains slowly after you already cleared it may have a different problem, such as a partial collapse behind the wall or a leaking drain line below the floor. We include a quick look at the drain pipe when the clog returns, because water leaking from a drain line can stain ceilings below and encourages mold.

If you notice a wet spot that grows when the drain runs, mention it on the call. That points toward a leak, and leak detection is the better next step than more cabling.
