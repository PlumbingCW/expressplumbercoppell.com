When a toilet overflow is an emergency

If water is rising, close the shutoff valve on the wall behind the toilet. Lift the tank lid and lower the flapper so no more water enters. Put towels around the base and keep people out of the area if the water looks dirty.

If other drains are also slow, the problem may be the main line. Do not keep flushing. Call us and we will find out. See drain cleaning if the stoppage is wider than one toilet.

Why toilets run, leak and clog

A running toilet is usually a worn flapper, a fill valve that no longer shuts off or a chain that is too short. A leak at the base is often a failed wax ring or loose closet bolts. A toilet that clogs repeatedly may have a partial blockage in the trap or a poor flush from mineral buildup in the rim jets.

Each of these is a different repair. We check the cheap part first and tell you if it is time to replace the toilet instead.

Replacing a toilet

If the porcelain is cracked or the toilet is 25 years old and wasting water, replacement is often smarter than another repair. We remove the old unit, check and repair the flange, set a new wax ring and install the new toilet level, then test for leaks.

Bring your height and layout preferences. Elongated bowls and comfort height seats are common. Rough-in distance, the gap from the wall to the drain center, decides what fits. See plumbing installation for other fixtures.
