Water spraying under the sink: do this first

Look for the two small valves under the sink, called angle stops. Turn each clockwise to close. If they do not move or keep leaking, close the main valve for the house and call us.

Empty the cabinet, put a pan under the leak and dry the area. Water on a wood cabinet floor can swell it fast. If a valve does not shut at all, that is the exact situation that calls for an emergency visit.

Common faucet and valve faults

A drip at the spout means a worn cartridge, seat or washer. Water at the base of the faucet means a failed O-ring. Low flow often comes from a clogged aerator, and a handle that feels loose may need a new set screw.

Shutoff valves under sinks can seize if they have not been turned in years. When they fail, they cannot isolate the fixture, which turns a small repair into a whole-house shutdown. We replace them as part of the visit when needed.

Repair or replace the faucet

A faucet that is 15 years old and has needed several repairs is a good candidate for replacement. A newer faucet that only needs a cartridge should be repaired. We tell you which makes sense and let you choose.

If you want a new faucet or sink installed, our plumbing installation page covers the work. Bring your own fixture or ask us to supply one.

Hard water and fixture wear

Coppell's water quality report does not list hardness, so we do not make claims about your tap. White crust on aerators and faucet bodies is a sign of mineral buildup, and a home test strip will tell you more. If buildup is heavy, ask about water softener and filtration.

Protecting your cabinet and floor from a slow leak

A slow drip under a sink can run for months before anyone looks. It soaks the cabinet floor, swells the wood and encourages mold. Put a small tray or a plastic liner under the sink to catch drips and reveal them early. Check it when you take out the trash.

If you find stains or soft wood, call even if the leak seems small. We can find where the water comes from and repair the part, and you can then decide what to do about the cabinet.
