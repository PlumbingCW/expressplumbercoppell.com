About Coppell Express Plumbing

Coppell Express Plumbing is a plumbing company at 115 W Sandy Lake Rd in Coppell, Texas. We answer the phone day and night and send a licensed and insured plumber for the repairs that cannot wait.

What we do

We handle emergency and planned plumbing in Coppell, including drain and sewer problems, leaks, water heaters, fixtures and pipe work. See our full list of plumbing services to find the page that matches your problem.

We also serve nearby Irving, Carrollton and Lewisville. Our service areas page shows which ZIP codes we cover.

Most of our calls fall into a few groups: water where it should not be, drains that will not clear, hot water that has stopped, and fixtures that fail. A smaller group is planned work, such as a new heater, a camera look at a sewer line before a home purchase or new shutoff valves under a sink. We handle both kinds from the same phone line.

How we work

You call, a dispatcher answers and asks what is happening. A plumber diagnoses the problem and gives you a price before work starts. If a job does not need an emergency visit, we say so and book the earliest window that suits you.

We stand behind plain statements and avoid vague promises. We do not promise an arrival time, because traffic and the nature of each call differ. We tell you what we know and when we will call back.

Licensing and checking us out

Our work is done by licensed and insured plumbers. In Texas, you can check any plumber through the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners public license search, using the Responsible Master Plumber number on the estimate. We encourage that for every contractor you hire.

What happens when you call

A dispatcher answers and asks four things: your address and ZIP code, what is happening, whether water is still flowing and whether anyone is in danger. If water is flooding, we talk you through closing the main valve. If a drain is backing up, we tell you to stop using water. If you smell gas, we tell you to leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.

Then we send a plumber, tell you what to expect and call back with an update. When the plumber arrives, the first step is diagnosis. You hear what is wrong and what the options are, and you get a price before anyone starts work. If you decline, you decide, and we explain what to watch for in the meantime.

Why local knowledge matters in Coppell

Coppell grew after DFW Airport opened in 1974, and most residential land was built out by about 2000. Many homes are now 25 to 50 years old. That is the age when water heaters have been replaced once, supply lines show wear and buried sewer pipe may have shifted. We plan for that when we diagnose a call.

The soil is part of the story too. According to the Dallas County Master Gardeners, the region's clay soil swells when wet and shrinks when dry, and that movement stresses buried pipe and slab lines. Coppell buys its drinking water from Dallas Water Utilities, and the city does not publish a hardness number in its water quality report, so we recommend a home test strip before anyone buys treatment equipment.
