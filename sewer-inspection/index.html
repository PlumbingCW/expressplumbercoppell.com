Sewer Camera Inspection in Coppell, TX

Sewer camera inspection in Coppell, TX puts a small waterproof camera down your drain line and shows what is inside, in video you can keep. It is the quickest way to learn whether a recurring clog is roots, grease, a belly or a broken pipe.

What the camera shows

A flexible rod carries a camera and light through the cleanout. The picture appears on a screen as the camera moves, and a locator on the surface marks where each problem sits and how deep it is. We note joints, cracks, roots, scale, bellies and any change in pipe material.

You see the video as it plays and receive a copy. That removes guesswork when a repair is recommended, and it gives you something to show an insurer, a buyer or the city.

When a camera inspection is worth it

  • ✓You are buying a home with a lateral older than 25 years.
  • ✓A drain has been cleared more than once in a year.
  • ✓Your yard has a soft or sunken spot over the line.
  • ✓You want to verify a repair or a lining job.
  • ✓You are selling and want to know what a buyer may find.

Buying or selling a Coppell home

A general home inspection normally does not run a camera through the sewer. Homes in Coppell were mostly built after 1974 and fully built out by about 2000, which puts the lateral at 25 to 50 years old. That is old enough for roots and joint wear to matter.

A camera inspection before closing gives you an honest picture and some negotiating room. If you are selling, a clean report saves a stressful surprise late in the deal.

How the clay soil figures in

Clay soil across the Dallas and Fort Worth region swells and shrinks, per the Dallas County Master Gardeners. Over the years that movement can offset joints and create bellies. The camera lets us see the effect and judge if it is minor or serious.

If we find a problem, we show it to you on the screen and explain the options. We do not recommend a repair that the footage does not support.

After the inspection

You get the video file and a plain-language summary of the findings, with the location of each and its depth from the cleanout. If the line is sound, you are done. If not, we explain the options and give a price before any work.

For lines with active clogs, see drain cleaning and the related service pages for the right fix.

How to prepare for an inspection

  • ✓Make sure the cleanout is easy to reach.
  • ✓Tell us about any past backups, repairs or roots.
  • ✓Avoid running water heavily just before the visit.
  • ✓Ask us to record the video so you can keep a copy.
  • ✓Have questions ready about anything the camera shows.
