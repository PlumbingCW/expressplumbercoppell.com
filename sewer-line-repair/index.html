Sewer backup: what to do first

Stop running water. That means no showers, laundry or dishwasher while a main line is blocked, since every gallon you add can come back up through the lowest drain. Keep children and pets out of wet areas.

Do not use chemical cleaners in a stopped sewer. They rarely help and make the work unsafe for the plumber. Call us, tell us where the cleanout is if you know, and note whether neighbors have the same trouble, which points to the city main.

Why sewer lines fail in Coppell

Clay soil in the Blackland Prairie swells and shrinks, and that motion can offset pipe joints, crack older pipe and open belly sections where the line sags. Tree roots find tiny gaps and enter. Older cast iron and clay lines also corrode or crumble.

Coppell was mostly built after 1974 and fully built out by about 2000, so many sewer laterals are 25 to 50 years old. That is old enough for joints to show wear, and it is why a camera look is smart before any dig.

Pipe lining, step by step

Lining starts with cleaning, often with hydro jetting, so the pipe wall is bare. A felt tube soaked in resin is pulled through, inflated and cured. The result is a pipe within a pipe with no joints for roots to enter.

Lining needs a pipe that still holds its round shape. A collapsed line or a deep belly full of standing water usually needs digging instead. We tell you which camera findings point to which method.
