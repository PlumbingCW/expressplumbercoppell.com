Riverchase Estates and the Fairways at Riverchase

Listing records separate Riverchase Estates from the Fairways at Riverchase, with streets such as Biltmore Court, Berkshire Lane, Coventry Court and Kingsmill Court appearing under Fairways. A real-estate guide reports observed build years of 1993 to 1997 in recent sales, and says select properties back to the golf course.

That is a narrower range than some Coppell areas, and it is only a sample. We do not claim every home was built in those years, and we do not know the exact boundary between the two sections.

Home size and layout

The guide reports closed sales from about 2,124 to 3,872 square feet with a median near 2,825 square feet, and describes features such as study rooms, game or media rooms and spiral staircases. Larger rooms and more floors mean longer pipe runs.

Ask your plumber about hot water delivery if a far bathroom takes a long time to warm up. Sometimes the answer is a heater setting or a valve. Sometimes it is simply the distance.

A school district detail to confirm

Historical sales records in the guide point to Carrollton-Farmers Branch ISD for Riverchase addresses, while much of the rest of Coppell is in Coppell ISD. We mention this only because it shows how the city's edges and its school districts do not always line up.

A short checklist for Riverchase homeowners

These steps apply to almost any larger two-story home.

Heater check

Read the age on the water heater label and look for rust or water at the base.

Far bathroom test

Time how long hot water takes to arrive at the farthest tap.

Drain pattern

Note which fixtures drain slowly and whether they share a wall.

Photos and notes

Photograph any stain and write down when it first appeared.
