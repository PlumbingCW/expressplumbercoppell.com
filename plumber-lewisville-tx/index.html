Freeze risk around the lake

Open land and water can make cold snaps feel sharper, and the 2021 North Texas freeze showed how many pipes in attics and exterior walls are exposed. If a hard freeze is coming, insulate exposed lines, open cabinet doors on exterior walls and let a faucet drip.

If a pipe has frozen, do not use an open flame. Warm air from a hair dryer is safer. If it has burst, close the main valve and call us.
