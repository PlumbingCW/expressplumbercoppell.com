A subdivision built in sections over several years

Listing data labels this subdivision by section, with Winding Hollow Lane in Section 1, Hampton Court in Section 4, Bricknell Lane in Section 5 and Loxley Drive and Blackfield Drive in Section 6. A real-estate guide reports observed construction years from 1986 to 1999, which means the sections were not all finished at the same time.

That range is a sample from closed sales, not a record of every home. Still, it makes one practical point. A house on Winding Hollow Lane and a house on Loxley Drive may have been built years apart, so ask each owner what they have.

Sizes and layouts vary here

The same guide reports 30 closed sales between late 2023 and mid 2026, with homes from about 1,578 to 3,487 square feet and a median near 2,752 square feet. Some layouts put the primary suite on the main floor, and others keep every bedroom upstairs.

For plumbing, layout changes where pipes run. A two-story house usually puts bathrooms above one another, and a single-story layout spreads them out. Tell the plumber how your home is arranged.

Creek and pond lots: watch the yard as well as the house

Per the real-estate guide, some lots in the subdivision are corner or cul-de-sac lots and others back to creeks or ponds. We know nothing about the drainage on any specific lot. A general habit helps anywhere near water.

  • ✓Notice wet or soft spots in the yard that stay wet after the weather dries.
  • ✓Check where downspouts and sump or drain discharge lines empty.
  • ✓Look at the foundation line for new stains or damp mulch.
  • ✓Compare your water bill with the same month last year.

Association rules and permits

Historical sales in a real-estate guide list annual association dues, so check whether your lot belongs to an association. Dues change, so we do not quote them. If your plans touch the exterior, ask the association what approval it needs.

For work inside the house, Coppell Building Inspections at 972-304-3500 can tell you whether a permit is needed. According to the city's permits page, water heaters have their own permit type.
