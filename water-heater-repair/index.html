Leaking water heater: what to do first

Water around the base of a tank is the call we get most. First find where it comes from. A drip from the relief valve discharge pipe or a loose fitting is repairable. Water seeping from the bottom of the tank shell means the steel has corroded through.

Close the cold water shutoff on the pipe above the tank. For an electric unit, switch off its breaker. For gas, turn the gas valve to off or pilot. Then call, because a tank that keeps feeding water will flood a garage or closet quickly.

No hot water: the usual suspects

Callers often describe the same few symptoms, so here is how we sort them. Lukewarm water from an electric heater usually points to a failed lower element or thermostat. No hot water at all can mean a tripped high-limit switch or a dead upper element.

On a gas heater, a pilot that will not stay lit suggests a worn thermocouple or a draft problem. Rumbling or popping sounds come from sediment baked onto the bottom of the tank, which also makes the burner work harder.

Gas, electric and hybrid heaters need different repairs

  • ✓Gas: pilot assembly, thermocouple, gas control valve, flue draft and burner cleaning.
  • ✓Electric: upper and lower heating elements, thermostats and the high-limit reset.
  • ✓Hybrid heat pump: condensate drain, air filter, compressor error codes and element backup mode.
  • ✓Every type: relief valve, drain valve, anode rod and dip tube.

How age and water conditions shorten tank life

Coppell buys its drinking water from Dallas Water Utilities, and the city's 2025 water quality report lists a total chlorine residual that averaged 3.10 parts per million. That report does not give a hardness figure, so we do not guess one. Ask the utility for its water quality report, or test your own tap, because mineral content decides how fast sediment builds in a tank.

Heaters installed when a Coppell home was new may already be on a second or third tank. Flushing once a year and checking the anode rod are the cheapest ways to add life. If your tank is past ten years and has never been flushed, repair money is often better spent toward a new installation.

Heater faults caused by cold snaps and outages

The 2021 winter storm showed how fast things go wrong across Coppell. Oncor reported about 11,500 Coppell residents without power on the Monday morning of the outage, and Coppell ISD closed for a week. Heaters in garages and attics saw frozen supply lines and tripped controls when power returned.

If your heater did not restart after an outage or freeze, check the breaker and reset button before calling. If water is leaking at a frozen fitting, our burst pipe repair service covers that.
